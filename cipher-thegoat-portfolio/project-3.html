VOID

Year
2026
Role
Motion Designer
Discipline
Motion Design

A motion design system for a digital-first cultural platform. The visual language emphasizes negative space, precise timing, and typographic movement to create a sense of considered emptiness.

Every animation was designed to feel inevitable — as if the elements could only move in that exact way.
